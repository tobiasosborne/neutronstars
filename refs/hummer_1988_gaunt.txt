                                                           GAUNT FACTOR FOR HYDROGENIC IONS

                                                                                       ABSTRACT
                                         A two-dimensional Chebyshev expansion of the nonrelativistic hydrogenic free-free Gaunt factor is present-
                                       ed which can be evaluated extremely rapidly and which gives a maximum relative error of 0.7% over a very
                                       wide range of temperatures and frequencies: —4.0 < log (hv/kT) < 1.5 and —3.0 < log (Z2 Ry/kT) < 3. This
                                       expansion is obtained from numerical values computed primarily from the exact expressions of Karzas and
                                       Latter, augmented by certain analytic approximations that are valid in regions of the energy plane where the
                                       series given by Karzas and Latter become computationally intractable. A brief table of thermally averaged
                                       Gaunt factors is given that covers the ranges of m = hv/kT and y2 = Z2 Ry/kT mentioned above in intervals
                                       of 0.25 dex.
                                       Subject headings: atomic processes — numerical methods

                                                                                       I. INTRODUCTION
                            Although the calculation by Karzas and Latter (1961 ; hereafter KL) of the Gaunt factor for nonrelativistic free-free transitions of
                         a hydrogenic ion has, for many years, provided the primary source for these data, the utility of their work has been limited by the
                         graphical form in which their results are presented. In conjunction with a new, ab initio calculation of opacities for stellar envelopes
                         by the “Opacity Project” (Seaton 1987), the need was felt for a fast and accurate method of generating the free-free absorption
                         coefficient for hydrogenic ions. Such a method would also be very useful in the modeling of stellar atmospheres and winds, where
                         previously a variety of approximations and functional fits to published graphical and tabular material have been used; these
                         procedures are of only moderate accuracy.
                            We have therefore used the expansion derived by KL to obtain primary values of the free-free Gaunt factor as a function of the
                         initial and final electron energies, augmented by various analytical approximations valid in certain regions of the energy plane for
                         which the expressions given by KL become computationally intractable. The thermal averages are computed numerically, and the
                         resulting values of ^ff(T, v) are fitted to a two-dimensional Chebyshev expansion in logarithmic temperature and frequency
                         variables. We believe our primary values to be accurate to better than 0.3%. Although we have obtained fits with a maximum error
                         of less than 0.01 %, we give here a smaller set of coefficients with an rms fitting error of 0.1 % and a maximum fitting error of 0.7%.
