                                                                                 ABSTRACT
                                      An effective absorption cross section per hydrogen atom has been calculated as a function of
                                    energy in the 0.03-10 keV range using the most recent atomic cross section and cosmic abundance
                                    data. Coefficients of a piecewise polynomial fit to the numerical results are given to allow convenient
                                    application in automated calculations.
                                    Subject headings: interstellar: grains — interstellar: matter — opacities — X-rays: general
