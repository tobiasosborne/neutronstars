                                                                                                 ABSTRACT
                                                    It is known that vacuum polarization can modify the photon propagation modes in the atmospheric
                                                 plasma of a strongly magnetized neutron star. A resonance occurs when the effect of vacuum polariza-

                                                 keV) propagating outward in the atmosphere can convert from one polarization mode into another as it
                                                 traverses the resonant density, ρres ≃ Ye−1 η −2 (B/1014 G)2 (E/1 keV)2 g cm−3 , where Ye is the electron

                                                 sion is analogous to the Mikheyev-Smirnov-Wolfenstein mechanism for neutrino oscillation. Because the
                                                 two photon modes have vastly different opacities in the atmosphere, this vacuum-induced mode conver-
                                                 sion can significantly affect radiative transport and surface emission from strongly magnetized neutron
                                                 stars.
