# Topic: interstellar extinction and X-ray absorption toward RX J1856 (audit A34 L3, GAPS 2.3 and 2.5; spine nodes pipeline.earth-photometry and pipeline.surface-parameters)

Problem. R5 compares a 123 pc render to HST optical/UV magnitudes; the optical extinction A_V toward RX J1856 is small (Ho 0.12 mag, Kaplan 0.05 mag, kept separate; see obs.approx-kaplan2011-extinction) and the X-ray N_H absorption shapes the soft spectrum. The project rule says published fits (CCM89 curve, Verner photoionisation fits, Wilms abundances) are stubs that must be recomputed or justified. Local sources: refs/cardelli_1989_extinction (scan), refs/predehl_schmitt_1995_nh_av (scan), refs/wilms_allen_mccray_2000_ism, refs/verner_1996_photoionization, refs/morrison_mccammon_1983_ism, refs/kaplan_2011_rxj1856_spectrum, refs/ho_2007_rxj1856_thin_atm, refs/burwitz_2003_rxj1856_xray.

Required argument:
1. Optical/UV: write the extinction model A_lambda = A_V [a(x) + b(x)/R_V] as printed (CCM89), evaluate it at the HST bands used for RX J1856 (take the band pivot wavelengths from the local Kaplan or Walter sources, state them) for A_V = 0.05 and 0.12 and R_V = 3.1, and print the magnitude corrections per band. Then argue what an own route would need (grain size distribution plus Mie theory, Weingartner & Draine 2001, Draine 2003, Bohren & Huffman; not local) and whether, given the 0.05-0.12 mag level and the HST photometric errors (quote them from the local sources), the own route changes any R5 verdict. Quantify: what A_V uncertainty would flip R5.
2. X-ray: write the photoabsorption cross section sigma_ISM(E) = sum_Z A_Z sigma_Z(E) with Wilms abundances and Verner fits as printed (quote the Verner fit form and one element's parameters with file:line); compute the transmission exp(-N_H sigma) at 0.2, 0.5, 1 keV for N_H = 1e20 cm^-2 (print numbers), and compare with Morrison & McCammon at the same energies. Then state the own route (Opacity Project photoionisation, Seaton 1994, not local; or Hartree-Fock-Slater) and its depth.
3. The N_H versus A_V link (Predehl & Schmitt 1.79e21): derive its role as a consistency prior only, never as a fit input, and give the implied A_V for the Burwitz/Ho N_H values with numbers.
4. Recommend the stub policy for M2 with explicit uncertainty labels, and list the exact sources to fetch if the own route is pursued.
