# Topic: the own parameter fit (audit item A34) that PRD requirement R5 depends on

Problem. Ho et al. 2007 (refs/ho_2007_rxj1856_thin_atm) fitted (T_inf, R_inf, z_g, N_H, B, column depth) to Chandra LETG spectra and HST photometry of RX J1856; the pipeline must not consume those outputs (truth/entries/obs.toml rows with provenance = code), because R5 compares a render against the same HST and Chandra data. Local data sources: refs/burwitz_2003_rxj1856_xray (LETG spectrum, blackbody fit, ObsIDs in Table 1), refs/kaplan_2011_rxj1856_spectrum (optical/UV photometry and the two-blackbody description), refs/walter_2010_rxj1856_distance (parallax 123 pc), refs/vankerkwijk_kaplan_2008_rxj1856_timing (pulse amplitude), refs/ho_2007_rxj1856_thin_atm, refs/potekhin_ho_chabrier_2016_* if present, refs/wilms_allen_mccray_2000_ism and refs/verner_1996_photoionization (X-ray absorption), refs/predehl_schmitt_1995_nh_av and refs/cardelli_1989_extinction.

Required argument:
1. Write the likelihood: Poisson C-statistic for LETG counts folded through ARF/RMF (state the form; Cash 1979 is not local, so mark it), Gaussian chi^2 for HST magnitudes in STMAG (obs.stmag-definition is in truth/entries/obs.toml), plus the pulse-amplitude constraint. Define the parameter vector and the forward model for each of the three surface models in PRD R4.
2. Identifiability and degeneracies: show analytically which combinations are constrained by X-ray spectrum alone (e.g. R_inf/d versus T_inf, N_H versus low-energy slope), what the optical adds, what the pulse amplitude adds, and what remains degenerate (z_g versus M/R). Give the Fisher-matrix structure qualitatively and, where possible, with numbers from the local data (Burwitz Table 2 parameters and errors).
3. Show with numbers that a single blackbody fitted to the X-ray data under-predicts the optical flux (the known optical excess for RX J1856); compute the predicted STMAG in F475W (or the band Kaplan uses) from the Burwitz blackbody at 123 pc and compare with the local measured magnitude, printing both and the factor.
4. Circularity: argue precisely why using Ho 2007 values would make R5 circular and what minimal subset of inputs is legitimate (distance, N_H prior from independent sources, etc.).
5. Minimal honest computation for Milestone 2 and a stub policy.
