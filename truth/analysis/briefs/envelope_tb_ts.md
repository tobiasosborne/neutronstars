# Topic: the heat-blanketing envelope relation T_s(T_b, B, Theta_B, g) (audit A29; spine node pipeline.envelope-tb-ts)

Problem. The surface temperature map of RX J1856 is set by the envelope relation; the database holds the PPP2015 fits (truth/entries/surface.toml, ids surface.ppp15-*), provenance fit, with reimplement route "own 1-D magnetised envelope solve (PPP2015 Eq. 38 + GH83 flux projection)". Local sources: refs/potekhin_pons_page_2015_thermal (PPP15), refs/potekhin_yakovlev_2001_cooling (PY01), refs/greenstein_hartke_1983_surface_T (GH83), refs/potekhin_1999_magnetic_conductivity, refs/baiko_yakovlev_1995_ephonon, refs/slattery_doolen_dewitt_1982_ocp, refs/potekhin_chabrier_2013_mag_eos and refs/potekhin_chabrier_2000_eos (EOS), and the surface.* entries.

Required argument:
1. Set up the plane-parallel, stationary heat-conduction problem through the envelope with the anisotropic conductivity tensor in a magnetic field inclined at Theta_B to the normal: derive the effective 1-D conductivity kappa_eff = kappa_par cos^2 Theta_B + kappa_perp sin^2 Theta_B (or the correct form the local sources print) and the flux equation F = -kappa_eff dT/dz; state what hydrostatic structure and EOS it needs.
2. Derive the Gudmundsson-type scaling T_s^4 proportional to g T_b^alpha in the non-magnetic limit from the radiative-conductive matching (Rosseland mean opacity, the sensitivity strip), as far as local sources permit; identify where the exponent 2.2 comes from.
3. Explain the magnetic case: classical (Hall) suppression of transverse conduction, quantising field effects, the origin of the pole-to-equator contrast, and why PPP15 needs a 2-D treatment near the equator; evaluate the error of the 1-D cos^2 projection (GH83) with numbers.
4. Inventory every microphysical input the own solve needs (conductivities electron-phonon and electron-ion, Coulomb logarithms, OCP structure, radiative opacities) and mark which are local, which are fits, and the depth of the recompute tree.
5. Give a numeric sanity chain: for T_b = 1e8 K, g14 = 2.4 (RX J1856-like), B = 1e13 G, compute T_s at the pole and the equator from the PPP15 fit entries (print the numbers), then argue what the own solve must reproduce and to what tolerance before it may replace the fit.
6. Stub policy: is a labelled PPP15 stub acceptable for the hero render? Quantify the effect on the pole-to-equator gradient and on R5.
