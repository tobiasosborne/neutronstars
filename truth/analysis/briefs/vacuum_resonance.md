# Topic: vacuum resonance and partial mode conversion inside the radiative transfer (audit A20-A23; spine node pipeline.vacuum-mode-conversion)

Problem. The database holds the vacuum-polarisation coefficients (PLCH04), the resonance density, the adiabaticity parameter and jump probability from Lai & Ho 2002 and Ho & Lai 2003, and v1 had a depth-resolved P_jump implementation (archive/v1, see archive/v1/notes and the git log subjects "Depth-resolved P_jump" and "Document Avrett-Krook attempt"). Local sources: refs/lai_ho_2002_vacuum_resonance (tex), refs/ho_lai_2003_atmospheres_vacuum (tex), refs/van_adelsberg_lai_2006_magnetic_atm (tex), refs/heyl_hernquist_1997_qed, refs/kohri_yamada_2002_vacuum, refs/potekhin_lai_chabrier_ho_2004_polarization, and the opacity.vacuum-* and rt.vacuum-* entries.

Required argument:
1. Derive the one-loop vacuum polarisation tensor coefficients a, q, m in the weak-field limit from the Euler-Heisenberg Lagrangian (Heyl-Hernquist closed form as printed locally) and show the reduction to the PLCH04 expressions; print the constant 0.0329199 (PLCH04) from your derivation and state its origin.

Derive the Landau-Zener jump probability P_jump = exp(-(pi/2)(E/E_ad)^3) (or the local form) from the two-mode amplitude equations across the resonance: write the coupled equations, the adiabaticity parameter gamma_res and E_ad in terms of the density scale height H_rho, and the conditions under which the Landau-Zener form holds (linear crossing, slowly varying coefficients). Mark the Zener 1932 source as not local.
4. Explain how partial conversion must be implemented inside the two-mode Feautrier transfer (van Adelsberg & Lai 2006 Sect. 3): mode-swap of the specific intensities at the resonance layer, the frequency dependence, and the effect of the resonance being inside or outside the photosphere for the RX J1856 parameters (compute where rho_V sits relative to the photospheric density for a thin hydrogen layer, with numbers).
